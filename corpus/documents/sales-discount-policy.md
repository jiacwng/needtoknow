Account executives and sales development representatives can give a discount of up to
22 percent on the list price without approval.

Discounts above that need the Head of Sales. Discounts on multi-year contracts above the limit
also need the CFO.

Never discount implementation services. Offer extra training days instead when a customer
asks for a lower price on services.

Record every discount and its reason in the CRM before sending the proposal.
