The external auditors issued their management letter for 2025 in May 2026. It lists seven
findings, none of them material.

The most important is finding AUD-25-F07 on revenue recognition: implementation fees were
recognised when invoiced instead of over the delivery period. Finance has changed the policy
from January 2026 and restated two quarters internally.

The other findings concern access reviews in the accounting system and the documentation of
manual journal entries. All are due to be closed before the 2026 audit.
