Business expenses are reimbursed when they are necessary for your work and approved by your
manager. Submit each claim in the expense tool within 30 days, with a photo of the receipt.

Train travel in second class is the default for trips in France. Flights need approval from
your manager before booking. Hotels should stay within the city rates published by Finance.

Meals with customers are reimbursed when the customer and the purpose are named in the claim.
Alcohol is not reimbursed.

Requests outside these rules go to Finance as an exception. Finance records every approved
exception and reports them to the CFO each quarter.
