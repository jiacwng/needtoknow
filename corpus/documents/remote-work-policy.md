Employees work from the office at least two days per week. Each team chooses its office days
and records them in the team calendar.

Remote work from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year. Ask your
manager and the People team before you travel, because tax and insurance rules depend on the
country.

The company pays a monthly allowance toward home internet. Equipment for home, such as a
second screen, can be requested through the IT service desk.

Customer meetings and team planning days take priority over remote days.
