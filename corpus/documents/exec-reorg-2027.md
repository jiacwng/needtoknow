From January 2027, implementation consultants and support engineers will move into one new
unit called Customer Operations. The unit will report to the CEO.

The goal is to shorten onboarding for new customers, a recurring reason for lost deals this
year.

The Head of People will prepare the contract changes and the communication plan. No employee
should hear about the change before their manager tells them in December.
