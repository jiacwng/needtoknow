The CEO set four objectives for 2027. Each one is tracked under its own objective code.

1. OKR-27-01: grow annual recurring revenue by 30 percent.
2. OKR-27-02: reach an EBITDA margin of 14 percent.
3. OKR-27-03: bring new customers live within ten weeks of signing.
4. OKR-27-04: keep employee turnover below 10 percent.

Each executive owns one objective and reports on it at every board meeting.
