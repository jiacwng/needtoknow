The CEO set four objectives for 2027.

1. Grow annual recurring revenue by 30 percent.
2. Reach an EBITDA margin of 14 percent.
3. Bring new customers live within ten weeks of signing.
4. Keep employee turnover below 10 percent.

Each executive owns one objective and reports on it at every board meeting.
