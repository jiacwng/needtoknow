Calder Systems has 54 employees. This page lists the leads and the people you will work with
most in your first weeks.

Elena Duval is the Chief Executive Officer. The executive team is Elena Duval, Tomas
Lindqvist (Chief Financial Officer) and Rafael Ortega (Chief Technology Officer).

Sales is led by Marco Bianchi, with Sofia Novak (Account Executive) and Ben Carter (Sales
Development Representative).

People operations is led by Chloe Martin, with Nadia Benali (HR Specialist).

Finance reports to Tomas Lindqvist and includes Lukas Weber (Accountant).

Engineering reports to Rafael Ortega. Amir Haddad leads the team of Julie Moreau (Software
Engineer) and Oskar Nilsson (Site Reliability Engineer).
