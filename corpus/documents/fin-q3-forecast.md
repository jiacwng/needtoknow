Finance forecasts Q3 2026 revenue of 4.62 million EUR, slightly above plan.

Subscription revenue makes up most of the quarter. Implementation services are lower than
planned because two customer projects moved to Q4.

The forecast assumes no customer leaves before the end of September. Churn risk sits with one
retail customer whose contract renews in October.

The final figures will be published to the executive team ten working days after the
quarter closes.
