Calder WMS is our warehouse management system. Customers use it to receive goods, store them,
pick orders and ship them.

The product has three tiers. Starter covers one site and basic stock control. Pro adds
multiple sites, wave picking and carrier integrations. Enterprise adds custom integrations and
a dedicated support engineer.

Calder WMS runs as a cloud service. Customers connect handheld scanners and their own ERP
through our public API.

Sales owns pricing. Ask the sales team for the current price list before quoting a customer.
