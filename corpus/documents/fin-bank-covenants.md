The growth loan signed in 2025 carries two financial covenants, tested at each year end.

Leverage: net debt must stay below 2.5 times EBITDA. Liquidity: cash must stay above
1.5 million EUR at all times.

A breach allows the bank to ask for early repayment. The CFO tracks both covenants monthly
and reports to the CEO when either comes within ten percent of its limit.
