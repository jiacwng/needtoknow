Vireo Retail signed a four-year Enterprise contract on 12 June 2026, covering nine
distribution centres in France and Belgium.

Total contract value: 1,240,000 EUR, invoiced yearly in advance. The contract includes a
dedicated support engineer and two custom integrations with their ERP.

Vireo can end the contract after two years with six months' notice. Renewal talks start in
the third year.
