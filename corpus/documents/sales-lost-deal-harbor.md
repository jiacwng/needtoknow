Harbor Foods chose Stockvale in August 2026 after a four-month evaluation.

Their team liked our picking module but found our onboarding plan too long. Stockvale offered
to go live in eight weeks and gave a larger discount on the first year.

Lessons: shorten the standard implementation plan, and involve engineering earlier when a
prospect asks about a specific scanner model.

This review is shared only with the account team and the executives.
