Commission is paid monthly on signed bookings.

Account executives earn 8 percent of first-year contract value up to their annual quota.
Above quota, the Summit Tier accelerator multiplies the commission rate by 1.6. Sales development
representatives earn a fixed amount for each qualified meeting that becomes an opportunity.

Commission is clawed back if a customer cancels within the first six months. Finance
calculates commissions from the CRM on the third working day of each month.
