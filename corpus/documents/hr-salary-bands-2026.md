These bands apply from 1 March 2026 and were approved by the executive team in January.

Engineering. Software Engineer (band E2): 46,000 to 58,500 EUR. Senior Software Engineer
(band E3): 78,400 to 94,100 EUR. Engineering Lead (band E4): 96,000 to 118,000 EUR.

Sales. Sales Development Representative (band S1): 36,500 to 44,000 EUR plus commission.
Account Executive (band S2): 52,000 to 63,000 EUR plus commission.

Finance and People. Accountant (band F2): 44,500 to 55,000 EUR. HR Specialist (band P2):
41,000 to 50,500 EUR.

Do not share these figures outside the People team and the executive team.
