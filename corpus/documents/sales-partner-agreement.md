We signed a referral agreement with Meridian Integrators in May 2026. They install warehouse
hardware and recommend software to their customers.

Meridian receives a referral fee on the first-year subscription of every customer they bring.
They also get access to our partner sandbox and two training places per quarter.

The agreement runs for one year and renews automatically. Marco Bianchi manages the
relationship.
