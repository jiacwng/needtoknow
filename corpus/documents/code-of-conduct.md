We expect every employee to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect.
Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

Report a concern to your manager, to the People team or through the confidential reporting
form. Reports are handled by the Head of People and are shared only with the people who need
them to resolve the case.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is a serious breach of this
code.

Gifts from customers or suppliers above 50 EUR must be declared to Finance.
