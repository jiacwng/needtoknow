We signed a three-year committed use agreement with our cloud provider in April 2026.

The agreement commits us to 38,500 EUR of spend per month. In return we receive a discount on
compute and storage compared with on-demand prices. Spend above the commitment is billed at
the discounted rate.

If our usage falls below the commitment, we still pay the committed amount. Finance reviews
actual usage with the CTO every month.

The contract terms are confidential under the agreement with the provider.
