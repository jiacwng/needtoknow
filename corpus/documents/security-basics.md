Use the company password manager for every work account. Turn on multi-factor
authentication for email, the HR portal and the code platform.

Lock your screen when you leave your desk. Do not store customer data on personal devices or
personal cloud accounts.

Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button. Report a lost laptop or phone to
the IT service desk the same day.

Engineering runs a penetration test every year. Findings are handled by the engineering
leadership and are not shared outside the people who fix them.
