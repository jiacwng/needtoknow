Every employee receives a laptop on the first day. Engineers can choose between two laptop
models with more memory.

Request a replacement, a headset or a second screen through the IT service desk. Standard
requests are delivered within one week.

Laptops are encrypted and managed by the company. Do not install software that is not on the
approved list without asking the service desk.

Return all equipment to the People team on your last day.
