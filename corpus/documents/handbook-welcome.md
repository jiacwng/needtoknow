Calder Systems builds warehouse management software for mid-sized logistics companies in
Europe. We were founded in Lyon in 2017 and employ people across sales, engineering, finance
and people operations.

Our office is at 14 quai Perrache, Lyon. It is open from 8:00 to 19:00 on working days. Badges
are issued by the People team on your first day.

Your manager is your first contact for anything about your work. The People team handles
contracts, leave and benefits. Finance handles expenses and payroll questions. Engineering
runs the internal IT service desk.

Read the holiday policy, the remote work policy and the security basics during your first
week. Ask your manager for access to the tools your team uses.
