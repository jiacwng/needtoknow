The draft operating budget for 2027 totals 11.8 million EUR, an increase of 12 percent over
2026.

Personnel costs are the largest line at 7.9 million EUR, including the nine planned hires.
Cloud hosting and software tools account for 1.3 million EUR. Office costs, including the new
office, account for 0.9 million EUR. Marketing and events account for 0.8 million EUR.

The board will vote on the budget in December. Until then, department heads receive only
their own lines.
