The 2027 plan adds nine roles, subject to the final budget.

Engineering gets four roles: two software engineers, one site reliability engineer and one
product designer. Sales gets three roles focused on Spain and Portugal. Finance and People get
one role each.

The Iberian sales roles and two of the engineering roles will be based in a new office in
Porto, opening in the second quarter of 2027. The People team is comparing two coworking
spaces and will present them to the executive team in November.

Hiring managers must not announce the new office before the executive team does.
