Pay is reviewed once a year. Reviews take place in February and new salaries apply from
1 March.

Each role belongs to a salary band. The bands are set by the People team with Finance and are
approved by the executive team. Your manager can tell you which band your role is in.

The review looks at your performance rating, your position inside the band and the market
data for your role. Promotions move you to the band of your new role.

Questions about your own pay go to your manager first, then to the People team.
