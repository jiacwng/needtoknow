Every full-time employee receives 27 working days of paid leave per calendar year, plus the
French public holidays. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contract.

Request leave in the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day off. Your manager
approves or declines the request within five working days.

Up to five unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on
31 March. Leave cannot be paid out except when an employee leaves the company.

The office closes between Christmas and New Year. Those days count as leave unless your team
runs an on-call rota during the closure.
